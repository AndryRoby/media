Puzzle for Adults on a Quiet Winter Evening: Mystery Dot to Dot Volume 4

Thirty pictures stay secret until you join the dots, so Volume 4 makes a slow, screen-free evening puzzle for adults and teens alike. It runs from 105 to 311 dots per puzzle, with animals, objects, food and vehicles in the mix. Start at dot 1, and at every star stop and begin again with the following number. Each puzzle fills a full A4 or US Letter page.
