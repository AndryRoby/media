Christmas Detective Game for Families: Who Ate the Christmas Cookies?

A printable Christmas party game for 3 to 10 players: one player is the Detective, everyone else is a Witness who saw one true thing about the cookie eater and may only act it out, then say one word, then three words. 30 cases for kids 8 and up, teens and adults. Not a murder mystery. PDF in A4 and US Letter, color or ink saver. The 16 suspect portraits were made with an AI image tool.
